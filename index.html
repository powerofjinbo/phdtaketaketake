Connection-first CAPEG scoring

Your advisor's network, lab pedigree, and letters are scored ahead of raw metrics — because that is how committees actually read applications. Signal flows to where it matters, not where it is easiest to count.

Evidence-cited research agent

A research agent reads primary sources for every candidate advisor. Every signal it uses is cited; anything it cannot verify is flagged, never invented — intelligence, not guesswork.

In-browser scoring engine

Missing or blocked sources widen the confidence band instead of being guessed, so rankings expose the lower bound. The engine itself runs in your browser (Python via Pyodide) — your data never leaves this device.
